Entry for the [Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-famous-last-words-the-funniest-epitaph-ff177406)
competition on make.fun: the funniest one-line famous last words, a tombstone
epitaph of at most 150 characters, judged pairwise by an AI graveyard judge
on whether it really is a one-line epitaph, whether it earns a real laugh,
whether it turns on a surprise, whether it reads like something carved in
stone, and whether it stays in the head.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the line to
`/output/entry.txt`. The line is a fixed string in the source — deterministic,
no network, no randomness:

> Here lies a man who said 'watch me' — and we did.

A daredevil's epitaph in one breath: "watch me" is the archetypal famous
last words, and the twist is that the mourners took him at his word — they
did watch, and they are still standing here watching. Short, carved-stone
plain, and quotable.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
